stopped states. Do not use purple, blue-black, neon, patients, diagnosis,

robots, AI brains, chatbots, holograms, text-heavy flowcharts, logos, or a
watermark.

The image must communicate synthetic-only data, hard stops before drafting,
typed draft without approval authority, schema validation before review, and
the final lever belonging to the human outside the chamber.
